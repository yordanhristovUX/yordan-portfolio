## Subtitle {#subtitle}

A concept project with no client behind it: a unified app for crypto and fiat payments,
transfers, and simplified stock trading, taken from research to a full Figma prototype.

## The exercise {#context}

Self-initiated, to work through a problem the leading fintech apps leave open: crypto and
fiat live in disjointed experiences, and moving between them is slow and confusing. I studied
the foremost apps in the field and talked with users about where they struggle.

## The design {#approach}

An information architecture that bridges the two realms: crypto transactions and trading,
deposits and withdrawals, fiat payments and transfers, all reachable within a few clicks,
with the user choosing their preferred mode of exchange. 50+ Figma components and variants, a
minimal typographic system and palette, and a working Figma prototype.

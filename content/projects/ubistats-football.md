## Summary {#summary}

Research → design system → UI → usability testing. Led to a Bulgarian Football Union
partnership.

## Subtitle {#subtitle}

Ubitrack, a tablet-first football analytics platform with dashboard mechanics and statistical
visualisations. I owned the whole arc: competitor, domain and user research, wireframes,
design system, UI, interactive prototype, usability testing. The platform became the basis
for the client's strategic partnership with the Bulgarian Football Union.

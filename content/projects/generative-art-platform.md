## Summary {#summary}

A concept study, not client work: an artist discovery platform for the NFT community,
designed full scope from IA to UI.

## Subtitle {#subtitle}

A concept project, not a client brief: a hub that gathers generative artists' scattered
presence into one place, designed end to end from information architecture to full UI.

## The exercise {#context}

Generative artists drive much of the NFT art scene, and their presence is fragmented: bios,
works, achievements and artistic statements scattered across personal pages, articles and
Twitter. Collectors and enthusiasts have no consolidated view; emerging artists have nowhere
structured to learn from the pioneers.

## The design {#approach}

A central hub with artist profiles collating bio, achievements, statements and sample works,
with direct links out to the artworks. A featured-artists view for swiping through rotating
profiles, an artist list for systematic discovery, and a high-contrast quick-access bottom
menu for the fast-paced, Twitter-native audience.

## Summary {#summary}

First-ever mobile app for Bulgaria's leading lifestyle media brand. Shipped.

## Subtitle {#subtitle}

Go Guide has covered Bulgarian city life for over twenty years; this was its first mobile
app. A digital guide to curated restaurants, bars, cafés and venues across Sofia, Plovdiv and
the Black Sea coast.

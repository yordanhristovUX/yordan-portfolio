## Highlights {#outcome}

- Introduced design systems as studio practice. No client asked for one — I built a
  white-label token-based system and reused it across projects, cutting setup time on every new
  brief and giving developers one source for spacing, colour, and type.
- **Ubistats Football** — analytics platform taken from research through design system, UI, and
  usability testing. Led to a partnership with the Bulgarian Football Union.
- **Spetema** — a 200+ product coffee catalogue serving households and businesses from one
  site. Split navigation into home and business tracks, layered a preparation-method finder
  over the category taxonomy, and structured eight sub-brands on a shared attribute model.
  Bilingual, live.
- **Mindromeda** — mental health marketplace connecting people to licensed therapists and
  coaches. Full design and IA: specialist matching by need, session booking, and consumer
  access alongside corporate vouchers. Live on Google Play.
- **Malko Tarnovo Municipal App** — semantic colour tokens and a dual visual language serving
  tourists and older residents at once. Live on the App Store and Google Play.
- Also: the USD bank account feature for Tax Advice, a cross-border tax refund service,
  designed end to end from proposition to launch; Go Guide, the first mobile app for Bulgaria's
  leading lifestyle media brand; and an internal order management UI for a top US auto parts
  distributor.

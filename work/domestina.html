Cleaning Service Marketplace

Domestina.bg

A research-led, multi-year UX engagement on Bulgaria's leading cleaning services marketplace — every design change grounded in evidence, not assumption.

UX Researcher & DesignerUX ResearchData-drivenMarketplaceMulti-year

The brief

Three user types, three definitions of success: clients booking a service and deciding who to trust, service providers competing on reputation, and housemaids needing a low-friction onboarding path. The challenge: serve all three without making any of them feel second-class.

Research

Competitor analysis showed Domestina had no direct comprehensive competitor in the Bulgarian market — it had to define its own UX standards. Personas built from interviews, support tickets, Google Analytics, and Hotjar surfaced the critical insight: clients were anxious about inviting a stranger into their home. Trust signals weren't nice-to-haves — they were the primary conversion driver.

What I changed

  • Front page redesign — restructured around the primary client action, trust signals moved above the fold, provider recruitment split into its own entry point.
  • Domestina Club — loyalty mechanics designed into existing flows without adding friction to conversion.
  • Provider workshops — made performance feedback clear and actionable, improving the whole provider pool.
  • Housemaid registration — progressive disclosure with named stages and visible momentum.

Measured outcomes

−40%

  • 40% reduction in front page bounce rate — from a focused hierarchy redesign, not a full rebuild.
  • Marked increase in completed housemaid registrations.
  • Improved retention following the Domestina Club launch.
  • Stronger provider performance after workshop-driven design changes.

Not a single project — a continuous improvement methodology applied to a live, growing marketplace over multiple years.

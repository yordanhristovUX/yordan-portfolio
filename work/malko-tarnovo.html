Municipal Mobile App

Municipality of Malko Tarnovo

End-to-end design of a cross-platform civic app serving two opposing audiences — tourists and older residents — live on the App Store and Google Play.

Product DesignerShippedDesign SystemiOS & AndroidWhite-labelAccessibility

The challenge

Two audiences with almost opposing needs. Tourists are explorers — visual richness, maps, discovery flows. Local residents, many of them older, need municipal services: waste schedules, announcements, contacts — with large touch targets, minimal cognitive load, and zero ambiguity.

The dual colour logic

Green encodes everything tourism — nature reserves, trails, gastronomy — connecting to the Strandzha region's identity. Blue encodes everything administrative, carrying the institutional calm of government services.

This is not decoration; it's a wayfinding system. A user glancing at a card knows instantly whether it's a tourist recommendation or a municipal service — before reading a word. For older users, colour becomes a cognitive shortcut.

White-label design system

  • Semantic colour tokens — swap the green/blue for any municipality's brand and it cascades through the entire system.
  • Spacing and rounding tokens — no arbitrary values anywhere in the library.
  • Typography system — Comfortaa, tokenised scale, friendly yet official.
  • Icon system — municipal logo redesigned on a 64×80 grid, coherent from app icon to in-app header.

Accessibility by default

  • Touch targets exceeding WCAG's 44×44px minimum throughout.
  • WCAG 2.1 AA contrast across both colour contexts.
  • Hierarchy through size, weight, and spacing — never colour alone.
  • Progressive disclosure for dense municipal content.

Outcomes

Launched on iOS and Android, publicly available on both stores. The white-label system is a reusable product — any Bulgarian municipality can deploy it with brand-token changes alone. The dual colour logic became a reference model for designing civic services for mixed-age audiences.
